# Pet Islands Adventure

A kids' adventure game in a single HTML file. Explore islands with pets that follow you, sail boats, fly, pick veggies from gardens, read books in museums, make friends, and battle your way to the Shadow Dragon.

## Play

Open `pet-islands.html` in any web browser. It works with a keyboard on a computer and with touch on a tablet or phone.

**Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to move, E to talk/use, F to fly, I for backpack, M for the map.

**Tablet:** tap anywhere to walk there, tap a person, animal, boat or door to go to it and use it, or steer with the on-screen circle. The big yellow button uses things and the blue button makes you fly.

Progress is saved in the browser automatically.
